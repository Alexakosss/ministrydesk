Questions

Good to know

Something else? Write to alexmovsisyan1@gmail.com.

Is MinistryDesk free?

Yes. The app, the updates and the cloud sync for your congregation are free to use.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Records are saved on the device first, so you can enter a month's data anywhere. When you reconnect, synced congregations update automatically.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in MinistryDesk's own database. If you sign in, your congregation's records also sync through your account so your PC and phone stay in step.

How do updates work?

Both apps check for a new version when they start and show an “Update now / Later” choice. Nothing is replaced until you confirm, and every download is verified before it is installed.

Can other helpers use the same congregation?

Yes. Each helper creates their own account and joins with the congregation's invite code, so records stay shared without sharing passwords.

Which devices are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) for the desktop app, and Android 7.0 or newer for phones and tablets.
